Privacy Policy

Bacteria Name Generator

Effective date: August 17, 2026

Bacteria Name Generator is a local utility that creates a playful, scientific-style bacterial name. We designed the app so that the information you enter can be processed on your device without an account or a developer-operated server.

Information you enter

The app asks for your first name, last name, and date of birth. These values are used locally to generate your bacterial genus and species name. They are not sent to the developer, uploaded to a database, or used to build a profile about you.

What we collect

We do not collect personal information through the app. Specifically:

  • There is no account registration or login.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics, or tracking technologies.
  • We do not collect names, dates of birth, contacts, location, health data, photos, or device identifiers.
  • We do not operate a backend that receives your generated names or entered information.

Local storage

The app stores limited settings on your device, such as the number of free generations used and whether the optional Pro unlock has been restored. The names and date of birth entered into the form are not saved in a developer-controlled account or database. Removing the app removes its local app data; an eligible Pro purchase can be restored through Apple using the same Apple Account.

PDF stickers and sharing

The optional 3×4-inch PDF sticker is generated locally on your device. It is not uploaded to us. If you choose Save / Share PDF Sticker, iOS displays its native share sheet. The destination you choose may receive the PDF and information included in it according to that destination's own practices. We do not receive or control data that you intentionally send through another app or service.

Pro purchase

Bacteria Name Generator offers an optional one-time Pro unlock for unlimited generations and PDF stickers. Purchases and restored purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment-card details. Apple may process transaction and account information under Apple's own terms and privacy policy.

External links

The app includes a link to the ASCLS Laboratory Week reference PDF. If you follow that link, your browser connects to the ASCLS website and its privacy practices apply to that visit. Bacteria Name Generator is an independent, unofficial project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by ASCLS.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app's behavior changes. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when a material change is made.

Questions

Questions or concerns can be raised through the project's public issue tracker.
